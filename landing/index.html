Everything, In One Place

Every module your workforce actually runs on.

No bolt-ons, no third-party integrations to wire up. Attendance, people and pay all live in the same workspace.

Employees

Add employees with an auto-generated ID, designation, salary and identification proof. Archive and restore without losing history — plus a filtered "My Employees" view for team leads.

Attendance

Mark Present, Absent or Double Shift yourself, or switch on Employee Clock-In so the team marks their own — with every record editable and overridable at any time.

Requests

Employees submit missed clock-in/out corrections or full and half-day leave applications with a reason. You approve or reject straight from the inbox.

Reports & Payroll

Monthly reports turn attendance into gross salary, overtime pay, advances and net pay automatically — export to CSV or print a clean PDF in one click.

Holidays

Set company-wide holidays once. Upcoming and past holidays surface automatically on every admin and employee calendar.

Designations

Define roles once — Manager, Sales Executive, Driver — and see live headcount, total payroll and average salary per role.

Geofencing

Add workplace locations with a latitude, longitude and radius — employees can only clock in from inside the fence. Set a working week per company or per employee.

Security & Sessions

See every active session and login attempt, cap concurrent devices, and require approval before a new device can sign in — with a push alert the moment it happens.

Company Settings

Manage company info, the admin account, appearance (light, dark or system) and legal pages — all from a single, self-serve settings hub.

Getting Started

Four steps from sign-up to your first payroll report.

This is the actual order every new AttendanceHub workspace follows — no hidden setup calls or onboarding tickets.

01

Register your company

Create a workspace with a name, email and password — you'll get a unique Company Code your whole team signs in with.

02

Set up admin & designations

Create the primary admin account, then add job titles in Designations so every employee record stays consistent.

03

Add your team

Each employee gets an auto-generated ID. Choose Admin Attendance or Employee Clock-In, and add geofenced locations.

04

Reports run themselves

As attendance rolls in, monthly reports and salary slips calculate automatically — export whenever you need them.
